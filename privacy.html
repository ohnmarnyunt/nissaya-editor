Nissaya Editor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Nissaya Editor (“the app”) is a desktop app for Windows and Mac. It reads photos and PDFs of Pāli–Myanmar books so that you can check the text and save it as Word, PDF or text. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

1. Your books stay on your computer

Everything you add to the app — photos, PDF pages, the text that was read, your corrections and the files you download — is stored only on your own computer, in the Documents/Nissaya Editor folder. The app does not send this data to the developer or to any server run by the developer. There are no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

2. Google sign-in and Google Drive

To read the text on a page (OCR), the app uses Google Drive’s free text recognition through your own Google account. When you click Sign in with Google, Google asks you to allow access to Google Drive with the drive.file permission. This permission only lets the app see and change files that the app itself creates; it cannot see any of your other Drive files.

For each page that is read, the app:

  1. uploads the page image to your own Google Drive as a temporary file;
  2. asks Google to turn it into text;
  3. downloads that text to your computer; and
  4. deletes the temporary file from your Drive.

The app does not read, keep or share any other information from your Google account. The images and text pass only between your computer and your own Google account; the developer never receives them. Google’s handling of these files is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your sign-in

After you sign in, the sign-in key from Google is stored only on your computer, in the system’s secure password store (Keychain on Mac, Credential Manager on Windows). You can remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Nissaya Editor’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to read your pages, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.

5. Deleting your data

To delete a book, use ✕ on the app’s Home page, or delete the Documents/Nissaya Editor folder. Uninstalling the app does not delete that folder, so your books are kept until you delete it yourself.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions: topsunshine@gmail.com
